Lower Moonee Ponds Creek · Both directions

Racecourse Road ↔ Footscray Road · The creek section of the commute, listed north to south.

Passability is unconfirmed at every crossing. Rain, high tides and drainage pump problems can flood the lower trail, especially between Dynon and Footscray Roads. Low rainfall does not mean a dry path.

No live water-level, tide, pump or closure data is connected. Check conditions and signs on each trip; do not enter floodwater.

⚠ Rainfall data is unavailable or incomplete. Rainfall risk is unknown.
