Creek and measured rain: Melbourne Water. Gauge timestamps have no UTC offset; interpreted as Melbourne local time. Creek heights use each gauge’s reference and are not path depths.

Tides: Ports Victoria / OMC. Breakwater Pier is a downstream proxy, not a creek sensor. Feed height datum is unspecified; do not compare its absolute height with creek gauges.

Modelled rain: Open-Meteo, upstream sample at 37.74°S, 144.91°E. Forecasts are separate from measured rain.
